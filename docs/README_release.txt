How to patch
  Use any BPS patcher (Flips, beat, or Rom Patcher JS) with {patch}.
  Start a NEW GAME after patching; savestates made with the Japanese ROM will not work.

What is translated
  All story dialogue, choices, battle text, speaker names, menus, help lines, item and skill names and
  descriptions, the item cards and skill labels, the location cards, the act title cards, the 7NEWS broadcast,
  the staff credits and end cards, the title screen subtitle and copyright lines, and the field signs.
  English text uses a variable-width font: dialogue boxes hold three lines instead of the original two.

Known issues
  - The katakana reading above the GetBackers logo and the 奪還屋 badge were removed rather than replaced (the
    logo already says GetBackers).
  - Some staff name readings in the credits are best guesses (Hiroshi Tanabe, Kenko Suzuki, Ikunako Nakahara,
    Kazumi Kitakami, Norihide Sugimoto).
  - The game's own English battle labels spell Kazuki as KADSUKI; they are left as the game drew them.
  - The 雷帝降臨 skill label reads "Lightning Emperor" (the label has room for two short lines only).

Tested in MesenCE 2.2.1 (cold boot, story events, field, menus, battles, credits).
