# Synopsis - GetBackers Dakkanya: Jigoku no Scaramouche

Source: `work/tl/story.txt` (steps 1-269 from `tools/debug/director.py`; act cards at steps 3, 55, 98, 123, 184, 209, 242).
Much late-act dialogue sits in the "unseen" part of story.txt (pointer order, ~line 4300 on); the plot below includes it.
Proposed spellings: `work/tl/terms_proposed.tsv`.

## Setting
Ura-Shinjuku (裏新宿), the shadow side of Shinjuku. Its underworld is kept in balance by the Ura-Tocho (裏都庁, shadow city
hall) and its five-member Internal Affairs Oversight Committee (内監委). A masked foreign investor, "Scaramouche of Hell",
is buying up the district's companies (Sazanami Corporation, BUN, ...) and gangs, and is pushing the gangs against the
Ura-Tocho. The heroes are always broke. Base: the cafe HONKY TONK (Paul, Natsumi), where Hevn brings them jobs.

## Plot by act
- **Prologue (steps 1-2).** TV news on the investor. Scaramouche questions the secretary Umakai and orders him to find
  "the boys". "I must have it... and for that I need their power."
- **Act 1 "GetBackers登場！" (steps 3-54).** Ban and Ginji flirt badly with a girl, then can't afford an 80-yen Park
  Burger (84 with tax: Ginji's "What IS consumption tax?!"). A schoolboy pays the tax if they get his game back from a
  bully, whose big brother beats them up. A white dog steals their burger; Shido is chasing it. Yamamura, a homeless old
  man in the park, feeds them. He lost his factory, and his daughter Rika was taken by the yakuza Haraguro Kogyo to work
  off his debt. Ban turns the job down (no money); Ginji goes anyway; Ban follows ("I owe him for the meal"). They break
  into the boss's mansion. **Twist (step 47-48):** the "captive" woman is Rika herself, now the boss's mistress, and she
  despises her father. The Chinese go-between Zhou names Scaramouche, who is looking for them. Ban's Evil Eye (step 52) makes
  the gang see hell; the police come. Ban's line: Rika is safe in jail and can see her father there. Hevn arrives with a job (step 53).
- **Act 2 (steps 55-97), haunted hospital.** At HONKY TONK, Sachiko Sudama hires them to open a "door" in the derelict
  Ura-Shinjuku West Hospital. Ban guesses it hides a dead director's fortune and asks for half. Ginji's VOLTS friend Yuya
  says he has joined the Dragonia-kai (his girlfriend Mie is against it). Ban and Ginji pay Yamamura back (step 62). Scaramouche, weak and
  in pain ("I must not fade yet", step 64), hires Himiko and Akabane (Transporters) and Hishiki "Undead" to test the
  GetBackers. The door needs the boss's giant maneki-neko; Hishiki takes it, Himiko and Akabane carry it to the hospital,
  Himiko is hit by the noise and joins the heroes (Akabane leaves: "the job ended at delivery"). **Reveal (step 93):** the ghosts are
  hallucinations from a modified Sazanami hypnotherapy machine (SCK-1). **Twist (steps 94-96):** Sachiko is the ghost of the
  director's wife: out of jealousy he shut her up in the hospital; the coffin is hers. "I can finally go home."
- **Act 3 (steps 98-122), Patty.** Shido has a job from Madoka: find Patty, the white Briard of Nao Imazato, a little girl
  with a heart condition who needs surgery. The trail leads to Kimoji's Pet Shop, a front run for the Ura-Tocho by
  Kimoji Kazama to smuggle protected animals. The parrot Paro (a running joke) tells Shido where the manager is if Shido
  says he loves it. Shido's Beast Mimicry beats Kazama. Patty is at the Park Burger stand, with a big unpaid
  burger bill; Himiko pays it. News: gang war is growing.
- **Act 4 (steps 123-183), Sazanami Corporation.** Kazuki (leader of the Ura-Shinjuku Junk Kids) faces Aono of the
  Ura-Tocho, who says Junk Kids people plan to kill the Ura-Tocho governor. Hevn's client Serizawa, a Sazanami engineer fired
  that day, wants a memo with his password from his old office. Inside, the new foreign owner (Scaramouche) and vice-president
  Kayama watch, and Scaramouche sets the robot Zagad on the team as a test. Fake mercenary Yagara loses. Scaramouche meets them
  face to face (step 162). The memo is in the cleaning lady's pocket, used as a tissue (the sticky-memo joke). **Twist
  (steps 168-180):** the password starts Serizawa's bombs. The countdown and the zombie are Ban's Evil Eye: Ban never handed
  over the memo. Serizawa's reason: Zagad was his security robot, made into a weapon on the owner's orders. Kayama
  takes him back and drops the weapon spec. Zagad's secret F Mode ("Final/Phoenix") still has to be beaten.
- **Act 5 (steps 184-208), Yuya.** Yuya disappears with a gun: the Dragonia-kai has sent him to shoot the
  Ura-Tocho governor at Ura-Shinjuku Station. Scaramouche did not order it and wants it stopped. Ginji chases Yuya through
  the station. **Branch:** either they talk him down (steps 196-200: he and Mie leave town together) or Ura-Tocho agents shoot
  him (steps 202-207: Ginji's rage, "Yuya's no terrorist!"; Ginji tells Mie he did it to prove himself to her). The epilogue has
  both versions of Mie (leaving with Yuya / "I'll work hard and live"). Trigger not checked: UNSURE.
- **Act 6 (steps 209-241), Ura-Tocho.** Hevn explains the Ura-Tocho and the Five. Akabane rejoins (Hevn hired him). The client
  Asia Mandel, a South American from a family of prophets/shamans wiped out in a coup, asks for "HOG", the clue to her
  people's lost "Divine Scripture" (神の記述). Ban works out that she is backed by Scaramouche; she re-issues the job in
  Scaramouche's name. **Reveal (step 211):** Umakai is Kudaru Hitoya (Gigoku), the Ura-Tocho's spy. Scaramouche offers all
  profits made in Japan (over 10 billion yen). Ban's terms: pay, then leave Ura-Shinjuku. They fight through the Ura-Tocho:
  receptionist Saemi, the OA-OLDERS, Gigoku, vice-governor Mitate, Akai and Aono. Governor Masurao Shikono (step 233):
  Scaramouche used them to wear down the Five. HOG = Hand Of God. After he loses he hires them to "retrieve
  Ura-Shinjuku's peace". **Twist (steps 235-237):** Scaramouche comes for HOG, but finding it was Ban's Evil Eye.
  Ban unmasks him as Asia, and **Scaramouche is a woman**. Her prophecy: "the young warriors of the East who draw near to the
  Hand of God". Her fading power came from the Scripture. Final battle. News: the investor has withdrawn and cannot be
  found. Hevn secretly took Scaramouche's fee and paid off the tab (step 238).
- **Act 7 "ACT OF..." (steps 242-269), bonus chapter.** Flashback (steps 241-244): months earlier Akabane carried off a
  document stolen from the Ura-Tocho, and killed Kida as Akai and Aono fled. Gigoku was moved up to fill the seat and sent,
  in disguise, after Scaramouche. Months after the main story Asia's lawyer delivers a letter: if she fails, the
  GetBackers are to retrieve HOG. Hevn says the fee was in the money she took. Back to the Ura-Tocho: Ginji and Akabane are
  separated by a trap ("the red and blue revenge"); Ginji makes Akabane promise not to finish them off. Masurao: HOG has no shape. It
  is a memory of the Scripture passed between chosen people, it has left his head, maybe it went to Mitate. Last fight.
  The mystery is left open: Hevn says she will solve it someday, Ginji wants food, Ban says it's on Hevn.

## Who is who (EN per terms_proposed.tsv)
- **Ban Mido** (美堂蛮). Cocky, crude, slangy (ねー/じゃねーか, コラァ, ったく); オレ (俺 late). Money-minded but soft-hearted.
  Calls Ginji 銀次, Shido "monkey trainer" (サル回し/猿マワシ), Hevn a jinx (ヤクビョーガミ女). Evil Eye: one minute of
  illusion; his line after it: 「悪夢（ゆめ）は見れたかよ？」. Snake skills (蛇*). Brags "nearly 100%".
- **Ginji Amano** (天野銀次). Sweet, earnest, whiny when hungry, gets furious at cruelty. オレ (僕 when soft or serious); calls
  Ban 蛮ちゃん (Ban-chan), Kazuki カヅっちゃん (Kazu-chan), Himiko 卑弥呼ちゃん. Ex-leader of VOLTS; makes electricity
  ("electric eel"). Always adds 「ほぼ」 ("almost") to Ban's 100%. Tare-Ginji = his droopy, worn-out form.
- **Shido Fuyuki** (冬木士度). Ex-VOLTS, Beast Master, lives with Madoka. Gruff, terse, オレ; carries no money; soft on kids
  and animals. Beast Mimicry (百獣擬態) skills ~擬.
- **Kazuki Fuchoin** (風鳥院花月). Leader of the Ura-Shinjuku Junk Kids, Fuchoin-style string art. Polite, desu/masu, 僕 (私 when
  formal); 銀次さん, 蛮くん. Mistaken for a girl ("white as a girl", step 164). Game label KADSUKI -> Kazuki.
- **Himiko Kudo** (工藤卑弥呼). "Lady Poison", Transporter; perfume skills ~香. あたし; calls him plain 蛮 (they have a past:
  「あんたのそーいうとこが昔から」); sharp, practical, pays the bills.
- **Kurodo Akabane** (赤屍蔵人). "Dr. Jackal", Transporter, "108 weapons". Very polite, 私, 銀次君, giggles 「クス」;
  catchphrase 「遊びの時間は終わりです」 (echoed by an agent and by Yagara). Ginji is scared of him (running gag).
- **Hevn** (ＨＥＶＮ). Broker. あたし, flirty 「お・ね・が・い」, calls Ban 蛮くん; keeps the money.
- **Paul Wan** (波児, label マスター). HONKY TONK owner; always asks about the tab (ツケ). **Natsumi** (夏実): cheerful
  waitress, 蛮さん. **Madoka** (マドカ): only mentioned.
- **Scaramouche** (スカラムーシュ) / **Asia Mandel** (アーシア・マンデル). Masked, cold, high register (私, ～のだ, ～ぞ, わが,
  きさまら), quotes Japanese sayings and explains them (蟷螂の斧, 蛇の道はヘビ). Takes the name from the commedia dell'arte
  clown her father acted out. As "Asia" she speaks carefully polite learned Japanese, then as cold as Scaramouche.
  **Until step 235 don't give Scaramouche a gender: no he/him, use "Scaramouche" or "you".** Asia's link to
  Scaramouche: hinted at step 210, said outright at 236.
- **Umakai** (馬飼) = **Kudaru Hitoya** (疑獄行使, "Gigoku"). Grovelling keigo as the secretary; as himself rude, オレ,
  「ひゃひゃひゃ」, a joke character (tone-deaf). Reveal step 211: keep Umakai simply loyal before that.
- **Masurao Shikono** (醜野丈夫). Shadow governor, the strongest psychic. Old-man speech わし/じゃ/のう/おぬし. **Mitate
  Shikono** (醜野御楯), vice-governor, his younger brother: coward, calls 「タイム！」, fights with minions, pet "Pochi" (μベビー).
- **Terasu Akai** (赤井太陽, treasurer) and **Hikari Aono** (青野月光, administrative commissioner): a red/blue comic pair with
  self-introduction slogans; Aono talks tough and runs. **Daichi Kida** (黄田大地): third colour/element, killed by Akabane.
- **Saemi Eileen Tamai** (玉井・アイリーン・沙恵見). Receptionist, "12 years an office lady!", "No sales calls without an appointment!".
- **OA-OLDERS**: Shredder, Time Coder, Copier (シュレッダー, タイムコーダー, フクシャー). Old men pushed aside by office tech
  ("workers' battlefield!"). Their chant puns on office life.
- **Yamamura** (山村). Homeless old man, western dialect (じゃけェ, わし, ～んよ). Daughter **Rika** (理香): bratty あたし.
- **Kumicho** (組長, the boss) and **Aniki** (アニキ). Kansai-yakuza speech (～や, しくさって). **Wakagashira** (若頭): "Assistant
  Acting Underboss", 15 years in the post (joke). **Zhou** (周): pidgin Chinese-Japanese (ワタシ, ～ね).
- **Sachiko Sudama** (須玉サチ子): very formal; the ghost. **Nao Imazato** (今里奈緒), **Patty**, **Paro** (katakana
  parrot talk with sound puns: テガカリ！アシガカリ！イイガカリ！, シド～ドレミファソラシド).
- **Kimoji Kazama** (風間蜥蜴児): pet-shop manager, smuggler. **Ryudo Hishiki** (菱木竜童) "Undead", ex-Ultimate fighter and bodyguard (護り屋).
- **Hiroshi Serizawa** (芹沢博史): nerd, ぼく, gloats in オレ. **Ikuo Kayama** (鹿山郁夫): vice-president, 私, comes good.
  **Yosuke Yagara** (耶柄庸介): "military adviser", claims to be an ex-mercenary.
- **Yuya Ujita** (宇治田裕也): ex-VOLTS, ～っす. **Mie Yuki** (結城美恵): his girlfriend, polite to Ginji, bossy to Yuya.
- Bonus cameo enemies 青樹佑夜 / 綾峰欄人 = the manga's creators (Yuya Aoki, Rando Ayamine). Also "???" with gibberish lines.

## Running jokes and wordplay
- Always broke and hungry: the 80-yen Park Burger (84 with tax), "35 + 45 yen", Paul's tab, the dog's burger bill.
- "Success rate nearly 100%": Ginji always adds 「ほぼ」 (steps 4, 212, and the Sachiko scene).
- 「悪夢（ゆめ）は見れたかよ？」 after every Evil Eye. After the game it is a fad: kids and an old man in the park say
  「いいユメ、見れたかい？」, and it is the end card. Use one fixed English line.
- 「奪られたら奪り還せ」 motto + 「奪還屋」 + 「ＧｅｔＢａｃｋｅｒｓ」 self-intro routine (steps 4, 31). Mitate gets it wrong as
  「ゲットだぜなんちゃら」 (a Pokemon "Gotcha!" nod).
- 「遊びの時間は終わりだ/です」 (Akabane, an agent, Yagara). Snake + electric eel duo.
- The Ura-Tocho's people run mid-fight 「タイム！」 ("they have a habit of running away", step 226).
- Field gags: the park Ojisan always wants a bath 「風呂入りてえ」 (and knows it); the old lady's 「はて、...？」 trivia questions;
  dozens of potted-plant (観葉植物) jokes (カンヨウ pun); mirrors; "can't eat it"; hospital no-smoking puns
  (吸いません/すいません, 吸わんのナミダ, 近縁の人から禁煙); Kazuo (park carving, the guard's toy-mad son, an overworked dev
  father, かずお「まだ、未完成だよ！」); Weekly Shonen Magazine always sold out; Pet Shop Snake's snake "Solid".
- Station lines: 裏渋谷, 裏池袋, 裏品川, 裏上野, 裏横浜, and 裏和 = Urawa (浦和) pun: keep the "Ura-" prefix.
- 「開発中！」 strings are debug leftovers (probably unseen).
